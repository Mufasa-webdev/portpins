If you are planning to visit Noumea, here are some of the must-see attractions and activities to include in your itinerary:

1- Stops at cannons:
On a small mountain with breathtaking views of the coastline.
Breathtaking views of the lagoon and the islands. View on the coast of Anse Vata and its beach lined with coconut trees.
View of Mont Dore, the highest peak on the mainland.

2- Stops at the centre culture Tjibaou / the visit inside for 45 minutes except Monday and public holiday.
This stunning cultural centre is dedicated to indigenous Kanak culture and features traditional architecture, art exhibitions and performance spaces.

3- Stops at the Church of miracles

Overall, Noumea is a beautiful and culturally rich destination that offers something for everyone. Whether you are looking for relaxation on the beach or outdoor adventure, you are sure to find something to suit you in Noumea.
