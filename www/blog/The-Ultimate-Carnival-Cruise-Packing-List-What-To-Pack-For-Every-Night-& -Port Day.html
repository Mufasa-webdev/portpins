The Ultimate Carnival Cruise Packing List - What To Pack For Every Night & Port Day

So you're going on your first Carnival cruise? I remember watching thousands of first-timers board on embarkation day — excited, overwhelmed, and making the same small mistakes that could have been avoided.

I'm not a travel blogger. I've worked onboard Carnival cruise ships for 10 contracts, living onboard for atleast 7 months at a time. I see what makes a first cruise amazing vs. stressful every single week. If my own brother was boarding for the first time tomorrow, this is exactly what I'd tell him.

1. Your Sail & Sign Card Is Your Everything

It's your room key, your wallet, your ID to get on and off the ship. You need it for everything. Don't put it in your pocket — you'll lose it. Get a retractable lanyard with a waterproof pouch and wear it from Day 1.

2. There Is Only ONE Outlet In Your Cabin

One outlet. Behind the desk. Not by your bed. Couples fight over it every cruise. Bring a cruise-approved power strip (no surge protector) with USB-C, plus a 10ft extra long cable and an extra charging brick. Security will confiscate any strip that says surge protector.

3. Magnetic Walls Will Save Your Tiny Cabin

Cabin walls are metal. Bring heavy-duty magnetic hooks (25lbs). Crew uses them to hang hats, lanyards, wet swimsuits, and excursion bags. It's the best cabin hack no one tells you.

4. Theme Nights Are A Big Deal - Don't Skip Them

Check your Carnival Hub App on Day 1. Every sailing has them, and if you don't pack for them you'll feel left out:

  • Formal / Elegant Night: Main Dining Room turns dressy. Men: collared shirt + pants. Women: cocktail dress. No shorts or flip-flops or they'll send you to buffet.
  • White Night (White Hot Party): Everyone wears all-white and dances on Lido deck under the stars.
  • 80s Night: The biggest Lido party. Neon, leg warmers, big hair. Pack 80s accessories - even just sweatbands and funky glasses.
  • Superhero Night: Dress as hero or villain. Kids love it. A simple cape and mask packs flat.
  • Island / Hawaiian Night: Tropical shirts, leis, floral dresses. Bring a pack of leis to share.

5. The Lido Deck Is Windy - Bring Towel Clips

Your towel will fly away every 5 minutes. Pack beach towel clips. They cost $1 on Amazon. No need to pack beach towels, we got lots onboard.

6. Bring Your Own Tumbler

Lido has free water, iced tea, lemonade, and coffee 24/7. Bring a 30oz insulated tumbler with lid and straw. It keeps drinks cold by the pool and hot in the morning. Bartenders will fill it bigger than the small ship cups.

7. You Can Bring Drinks Onboard (Most People Don't Know)

Carnival allows in your CARRY-ON (not checked bag): 1 bottle of wine/champagne per adult + 12 cans/cartons of soda per person. Don't try to bring liquor or beer - they'll take it.

8. Download The Carnival Hub App Before You Board

It's free and it works without buying WiFi. You need it for daily schedule, dinner menus, theme night times, checking your onboard account, and messaging other guests ($5 per person for chat).

9. Your Luggage Won't Arrive Until Dinner Time

Pack a small carry-on with swimsuit, sunscreen, medication, and anything you need for the first afternoon. Your checked bags can arrive as late as 8pm.

10. Pack 2 Swimsuits

So one can dry while you wear the other. Bring water shoes too - water shoes save your feet on rocky ports like Tangalooma Wrecks.

11. Formal Night Photos Are Free To Take, Not Free To Keep

Photographers will take amazing family photos on gangway, formal night, and with the ship background. Take them! You don't have to buy them. Prints start at around A$34.99 but you can just take photos of your own too. Bring a selfie stick with Bluetooth remote + flexible tripod for your own shots.

12. Port Days Need Their Own Ready Bag

Have a grab-and-go bag packed the night before:

  • 10L Dry Bag for Sail Card, phone, and cash
  • Snorkel set - you can snorkel right off the beach for free at Tangalooma
  • Reef-safe sunscreen + lip balm with SPF
  • Ponchos 5-pack - fits in your pocket for surprise rain

13. Don't Forget Seasickness Remedies

Even if you never get seasick, pack Sea-Bands + Bonine non-drowsy. The first night when the ship leaves Brisbane can be choppy. Better to have it and not need it.

14. Dining Room Is Free And You Can Order Multiple Appetizers

On first cruises, guests are shy to order. Don't be. You can order 2 appetizers, 2 mains, 3 desserts if you want. It's included. Same with buffet - go as many times as you want.

15. Cabin Doors All Look The Same

Bring a small door magnet decoration or fairy lights. It helps you find your cabin after a few cocktails and makes it fun for kids. Just don't use tape or anything that damages the door.

16. A Night Light Will Save Your Toes

Bathroom light is BLINDING at 3am. Pack a motion sensor night light. Crew all have them.

17. Your Phone Will Still Work In Airplane Mode

Put your phone on airplane mode to avoid roaming charges. Then turn on WiFi and connect to Carnival's WiFi if you bought it, or just use the Hub App offline. Take photos all day in airplane mode.

18. Gratuities Are Automatic - You Don't Need Extra Cash

Carnival adds daily gratuities to your account (about $16 per person per day). You don't need to tip extra at bars or dining unless you want to. Bring small bills only for port shopping and tour guides.

19. The Buffet Is Crazy On Embarkation Day

Everyone goes to Lido buffet at once when they board. Skip it. Go to Guy's Burger Joint or BlueIguana Cantina (on most ships) - same included food, no line. Or go to Main Dining Room for sit-down lunch on Day 1 - it's open and empty.

20. Laundry Is Expensive - Pack A Little Detergent

Bring 2 laundry bags to separate dirty clothes and a few sink packets if you want to wash small items. Laundry detergent cost money onboard.

21. Sunsets Are On The Other Side

On Carnival Australia cruises leaving Brisbane, sunset is usually port side (left) when heading north. Go to Serenity or top deck around 5pm. Bring your tripod - this is where you'll get your best photos.

22. Don't Overpack - You Live In Swimsuits

Trust me, 70% of your cruise you'll be in swimsuit + cover-up + flip flops. You need less casual clothes than you think and more theme night outfits than you think.

23. Door Decoration Contest? No, But It Makes Friends

When you decorate your door, neighbors stop and chat. Kids love it. It's the easiest way to make cruise friends. Bring extra magnets to give to kids down the hall.

24. Bring A Small First Aid Pouch

Just Band-Aids, Tylenol, Tums, and aloe. Don't bring a huge pharmacy. Pack it in a small pill bag. You'll thank yourself when you get a blister on port day.

25. The Best Memories Are Free

Trivia, comedy shows, dive-in movies on Lido, deck parties, Serenity sunset, watching Moreton Island sail past — all free. You don't need to spend money to have the best cruise. Save your money for one special thing — like snorkeling Tangalooma Wrecks or a helicopter ride in Airlie Beach.
