Mystery Island, Vanuatu: A Complete Guide for Cruise Passengers

Cruise Destinations Mystery Island Vanuatu

No city. No traffic. Just beach, culture, and good vibes.

If your cruise itinerary says Mystery Island, Vanuatu, don’t expect shops and taxis. Expect the opposite.

Mystery Island, also called Inyeug Island, is a tiny uninhabited island in the South Pacific that cruise ships visit as a tender port. It got its name from Queen Elizabeth II during a royal visit, and today it’s famous for one thing: pure, slow, tropical relaxation.

Here’s everything you need to know before you step off the tender:

1. Getting There: It’s a Tender Port

Mystery Island has no dock. Your ship will anchor offshore and you’ll take a small tender boat to the pier. The moment you step off, you’re already on one of the most beautiful beaches in Vanuatu. White sand, turquoise water, and coral right at your feet. No 30-min bus ride needed.

Pro Tip: Tender / Water Shuttle tickets go fast on sea days. If you have an early start, Book your water shuttle early so you don’t miss out.

2. What To Do on Mystery Island

This island is small, but it’s packed with “island things” that passengers love:

A. The Beach & Snorkeling

The water is crystal clear with healthy coral reefs just a few meters from shore. Bring your own snorkel gear and you’ll see fish instantly. It’s one of the safest, calmest beaches for swimming on any cruise. I haven't really seen a shop to rent or purchase snorkelling gear on the island as at the time of this post.

B. Local Village Experience

There are no major cities here, just a small local village. You can take a guided walk and learn how Ni-Vanuatu people live. It’s simple, authentic, and not touristy.

C. Massages on the Beach

Local women set up white wooden benches right on the sand and give amazing traditional massages. Ocean breeze + coconut oil + waves. 10/10 passenger favorite.

D. Hair Braiding with Beads

Want a souvenir you can wear? The women offer hair braiding and add little colorful beads. It’s quick, affordable, and looks amazing in photos.

E. Photos with Iguanas

Yes, real iguanas. Handlers will let you hold one for a photo. Kids and adults both love this. Don’t forget a waterproof phone case because you’ll be in and out of the water.

F. Live Music

You’ll be greeted by an amazing local musical group playing traditional Vanuatu songs. It sets the whole island mood.

G. Food & Drinks

  • Seafood Shack: Fresh grilled lobster, fish, and chips right on the beach
  • Coconut Water: Cracked fresh in front of you
  • Kava: Try the traditional Vanuatu drink made from pepper plant roots. It’s earthy and relaxing. Locals drink it during ceremonies. It won't get you drunk if that's a worry!

3. Hidden Gems to Look For

  • The Airstrip: Walk around and you’ll spot an old grass airstrip. Locals say it was built for small planes years ago. Great for photos.
  • Nature Vibes: With no cars, no buildings, and no crowds beyond cruise passengers, it’s the perfect place to disconnect and just be present.

4. Good To Know Before You Go

  1. Cash: Bring small AUD or Vatu bills. Most vendors don’t take cards.
  2. No WiFi: You’re truly off-grid.Download anything you need before you go, or grab an eSIM and stay connected.
  3. Shade: Palapas are first-come-first-serve. Get to the beach early.
  4. Time: Ships usually only stay 6-9 hours, so prioritize beach + 1 activity.

Final Thoughts

Mystery Island isn’t about shopping or big excursions. It’s about slowing down. Beach time, snorkeling, massage, fresh lobster, and connecting with local culture. That’s why passengers rate it so highly.

If you want a day that feels like a screensaver come to life, this is it.
