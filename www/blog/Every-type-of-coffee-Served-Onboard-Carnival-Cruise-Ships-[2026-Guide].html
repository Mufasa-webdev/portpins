Carnival Splendor: The Perfect Family Cruise Ship

Cruise Coffee Carnival

If you’re a coffee person, this is the most important Carnival guide you’ll read.

Because here’s the truth: not all coffee on Carnival is free. And the line at JavaBlue Cafe on sea days? Insane.

I am not much of a coffee person but i'll do it for you guys. After 30 Carnival sailings, I tried every single cup onboard so you don’t have to guess what’s included, what costs extra, and what’s actually worth it.

1. The Free Coffee On Carnival

Good news: you will not go without caffeine. Bad news: it’s basic drip coffee.

1. Lido Buffet Coffee Stations

Cost: Free 24/7
What you get: Regular + Decaf drip coffee. Sometimes hot water for tea.
Where: Every Lido buffet, plus Guys Burger Joint on some ships.
Verdict: It’s... cruise coffee. Fine for a quick wake-up before port.

2. Main Dining Room

Cost: Free with breakfast and dinner
What you get: Unlimited refills of regular coffee. You can also ask for decaf.
Pro Tip: Ask your waiter for coffee to-go in a to-go cup for morning port days.

3. Room Service Coffee Pot

Cost: Free coffee between certain hours of the day and after there is a charge. There is no delivery fee but always nice to tip your server.
What you get: A full pot delivered to your cabin. Takes 30-45 min.
Verdict: Only worth it if you have 4 people drinking it.

4. Casino Bar

Cost: Free while you’re gambling
What you get: Regular coffee served by cocktail servers.

2. The Paid Specialty Coffee: JavaBlue Cafe

This is where 90% of cruisers spend their coffee money. You’ll find JavaBlue Cafe on Promenade Deck on almost every Carnival ship.

Price: $4.95 - $6.95 per drink.

Full JavaBlue Menu

  • Espresso - Single or Double shot
  • Americano - Espresso + hot water
  • Cappuccino - Ask for "dry" or "wet"
  • Cafe Latte
  • Flat White - The most popular on sea days
  • Mocha
  • Caramel Macchiato
  • Cold Brew - New on Mardi Gras, Celebration, Jubilee
  • Iced Coffee & Iced Latte
  • Frappe - Mocha and Caramel, blended
  • Decaf - Available for any drink above

Add-ons: Flavored syrups $0.50. Extra shot $1.50. Alternative milk $0.75

Pro Tip:No need to bring your own tumbler. They’ll make your drink in a souvenir cup.

3. Other Specialty Coffee Onboard

1. Pig & Anchor Brewhouse

Found on Excel class ships. They serve Cold Brew on tap $6.50. Way smoother than JavaBlue’s.

2. Bonsai Sushi

Matcha Latte $5.95 and Japanese iced coffee $4.95. Surprisingly good.

3. Room Service Premium

You can order a JavaBlue latte to your room. It’s the drink price + $5 delivery. Only do this if you really need it.

4. What You CAN'T Get On Carnival

  • No Starbucks onboard
  • No Dunkin
  • No free specialty lattes - everything at JavaBlue costs extra
  • No coffee makers in cabins - they’re banned for fire safety

5. Carnival Coffee FAQ

Can I bring K-Cups? Yes! Bring a pack and ask room service for hot water.

Is JavaBlue included in Cheers Package? Yes. Up to 15 alcoholic drinks AND unlimited specialty coffee.

Best time to go to JavaBlue? Before 8am and after 3pm. Avoid 8:30am - 10am sea days.

Can I use my own reusable cup? 100% yes and I recommend it.

Final Verdict: Is Carnival Coffee Good?

The free coffee is fine. The JavaBlue coffee is genuinely good. Not Starbucks level, but better than most hotel coffee.

If you drink 2+ specialty coffees per day, just buy the Coffee Card on day 1. If you only drink 1, pay as you go.
