The Ultimate Carnival Cruise Packing List - What To Pack For Every Night & Port Day

Looking for the ultimate Carnival Cruise packing list that actually covers everything in 2026? I'm not a travel blogger — I live on the ship for months and see what thousands of guests pack (and what they wish they packed) every single week. From White Night to 80s Night, Superhero Night to Hawaiian Night, and from your cabin needs to your Tangalooma Wrecks port day bag — this is the exact checklist I'll send to my own family before they board.

Most first-timers pack only casual clothes and then feel left out on White Night or 80s Night. Let's make sure that's not you.

1. CABIN ESSENTIALS - Make Your Cabin Feel Like Home

Your cabin is cozy. Most cabins have just one outlet by the desk, not by the bed. These small items make a huge difference:

  • Cruise-Approved Power Strip (No Surge Protector) with USB-C & USB-A: So you can charge phones, watches, and cameras at once. Make sure it says "no surge" - Carnival confiscates surge protectors.
  • Extra Long Charging Cable (10ft) + Extra Charging Brick: The outlet is behind the desk. A long cable lets you use your phone in bed. Bring an extra brick - they are easy to lose in port.
  • Magnetic Hooks (25lbs, 4-pack): Cabin walls are metal! Hang your lanyard, hats, wet swimsuits, and excursion bag. Crew uses these every day.
  • 30oz Insulated Tumbler with Lid and Straw: Lido deck has free water, iced tea, lemonade and coffee 24/7. Perfect for keeping your coffee hot or water cold by the pool.
  • Small Decoration: Battery-operated fairy lights, a small cabin door magnet sign or magnetic dry-erase board - easy to find your cabin and makes it feel festive. Many families decorate their door for fun!
  • Night Light + Small Fan: Motion sensor night light for late-night bathroom trips, and a USB mini fan for airflow and white noise.

2. EVERYDAY & CASUAL WEAR

For sea days, Lido deck, and buffet - Carnival is casual during the day.

  • 4-5x Shorts / casual pants
  • 5-6x T-shirts / tank tops / casual tops
  • 2x Swimsuits - so one can dry while you wear the other
  • Swimsuit cover-up
  • Light jacket / cardigan - dining rooms and comedy club are always cold
  • Comfy walking shoes + flip flops / sandals
  • Sunglasses + hat

3. THEME NIGHTS - This Is What Carnival Is Famous For

Check your Carnival Hub App on Day 1 for your sailing's schedule, but these nights happen on almost every sailing:

A) Formal / Elegant Night (Usually Night 2)

Dress to impress! This is when the photographers get those beautiful family portraits.

  • Men: Collared shirt, pants, closed shoes. Suit or blazer if you want. No shorts.
  • Women: Cocktail dress, nice jumpsuit, or dressy top + skirt. Dressy sandals are fine.
  • Kids: Nice dress / polo and pants.

B) White Night (White Hot Party)

Everyone wears all-white on Lido deck. DJ, dancing under the stars. Very popular for photos!

  • All-white outfit: white dress, white linen shirt + pants, white top + shorts. Keep it cool - you will dance.

C) 80s Night - Back to the 80s Party

This is the most fun Lido party. Big hair, neon, leg warmers!

  • Neon t-shirt, scrunchie, leg warmers, high socks, fanny pack, funky sunglasses. Don't overthink - even a simple 80s accessories kit from Amazon with sweatbands and glasses works perfectly.

D) Superhero Night (Heroes & Villains)

Dress as your favorite hero or villain. Kids LOVE this one.

  • Superhero t-shirt, cape, mask, or full costume if you want. You can also just wear Marvel / DC colors. Simple superhero cape and mask set packs flat in your suitcase.

E) Island / Hawaiian / Tropical Night

Think tropical shirts, leis, grass skirts. Usually on the night before a tropical port.

  • Hawaiian shirt, tropical dress, lei, floral headband. Bring a pack of leis - you can share with table mates!

4. PORT DAY BAG

You'll want a separate bag ready to grab and go.

  • 10L Waterproof Dry Bag: For your Sail Card, phone, cash, and boarding pass on water excursions.
  • Snorkel Set with Dry Top + Mesh Bag: Tangalooma Wrecks has amazing snorkeling right off the beach.
  • Water Shoes: Rocky beaches and Wrecks can be sharp. Saves your feet.
  • Pack of 5 Ponchos: Small enough for your pocket if it rains during tour.
  • Reef-Safe Sunscreen + Lip Balm with SPF: Sun Bum 50 SPF
  • Beach Towel Clips: Lido and port beaches are windy.
  • Selfie Stick with Bluetooth Remote + Mini Tripod: Perfect for gangway photos and balcony sunsets.

5. HEALTH & COMFORT - Small Kit, Big Peace of Mind

  • Light Medication Pouch: Sea-Bands + Bonine non-drowsy for seasickness, Travel Tylenol & Ibuprofen, Tums, Band-Aids
  • Retractable Lanyard with Waterproof Pouch: For your Sail & Sign card - it's your room key, wallet, and ID. You need it everywhere.
  • Carnival Luggage Tag Holders: Paper tags rip off. These holders keep them safe.

6. Documents - Don't Forget These

  • Passport or Birth Certificate + Government ID
  • Boarding pass (printed + on Carnival Hub App)
  • Travel insurance docs
  • Carnival allows: 1 bottle wine/champagne per adult + 12 cans soda per person in carry-on (not checked bag)

7. My Quick Packing Checklist (Copy This)

Cabin:

[ ] Power strip (no surge) + 10ft cable + extra brick
[ ] Magnetic hooks 4-pack
[ ] Tumbler 30oz
[ ] Door decoration / magnets / fairy lights
[ ] Night light / small fan

Clothes:

[ ] Casual day clothes + 2 swimsuits
[ ] Formal night outfit
[ ] All-white outfit for White Night
[ ] 80s outfit / neon accessories
[ ] Superhero shirt / cape / mask
[ ] Hawaiian shirt / tropical dress + leis

Port Day:

[ ] Dry bag, water shoes, snorkel set, ponchos, sunscreen, towel clips, selfie stick

Essentials:

[ ] Passport, Documents, Lanyard, Med kit, luggage tag holders

Pro Tip: Lay out all your theme night outfits before you pack. Roll them, not fold them - you'll save half your suitcase space. And pack your port day bag last so it's on top - you usually dock the morning after embarkation!
